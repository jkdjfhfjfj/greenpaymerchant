name: Legal policy content
description: Rules for creating and publishing Greenpay Privacy Policy and Terms wording.
---

Only administrators supply approved policy wording. Never invent or publish legal copy.

**Why:** No policy wording was supplied; administrators must enter approved copy. The user selected a limited read-only dashboard-home exception before publication while keeping other workspace access gated.

**How to apply:** Keep legal drafts empty until administrators enter approved Privacy Policy and Terms text. Publish only that supplied copy. Until both current documents are published and accepted, allow administrators to manage policy setup and allow merchants only the limited read-only dashboard overview; gate other workspace routes and all merchant actions. Public-page formatting may render stored Markdown, but must not rewrite its wording.
